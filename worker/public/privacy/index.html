隐私政策

生效日期：2026-10-22

PixoTap 团队郑重承诺：仅为提供你选择的功能处理和留存最少必要的数据，对统计数据进行匿名处理。

工作记录

项目、会话、恢复笔记与相关材料引用保存在你的设备上。点击开始后，软件按照你的设置记录活动与 OCR 文字；截图原图、结束照片留在本机。剪贴板正文不作为后台记录保存。

AI 与文件工具

配置自己的 AI 服务密钥后，分析与伙伴对话会把必要的 OCR 文字、活动线索、对话、项目摘要和资料信息直接发送给你配置的服务。当前服务为 DeepSeek。伙伴使用文件工具时，读取出的文件文字、搜索结果和命令输出也可能进入后续 AI 请求，用于回答问题或完成你提出的操作。

截图像素和相机照片不进入 AI 分析请求。密钥保存在系统凭据存储中。第三方 AI 服务按其自己的条款处理收到的数据。

账号与同步

账号服务处理登录所需的邮箱、密码哈希、设备与会话信息。同步默认关闭。开启同步后，项目、恢复笔记、对话、偏好和 AI 密钥在设备上端到端加密，服务器只保存密文。截图、照片与原始工作文件不随项目同步传输。

你可以在账号中心管理设备、同意设置、导出数据和注销账号。注销有 14 天宽限期；期满后删除账号与业务数据，相关残留按服务清理流程处理。

匿名统计与运行记录

遥测默认关闭。统计接口仅接受已同意用户提交的记录，记录不包含账号标识，并过滤邮箱、IP、URL 与文件路径形式的值；保留 90 天。提供账号、安全审计和邮件服务所需的信息按具体用途处理，与匿名统计分别管理。

网站由 Cloudflare 托管；下载信息来自 GitHub；注册和找回密码需要邮件服务。这些服务会处理提供相应功能所需的网络请求或邮件数据。

浏览器存储

公开页面保存语言偏好与本次角色选择，下载页缓存公开发布信息。账号中心使用登录所需的会话 Cookie 与安全令牌。

数据留存与管理

本机项目由你管理和删除。服务器仅为账号、同步、反馈、安全与邮件处理保留对应数据。反馈正文端到端加密。你可以撤回同意、停用同步，并通过账号中心申请导出或注销。

联系我们

数据相关问题请联系 PixoTap 团队：pixotap@yeah.net。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-22

The PixoTap team commits to processing and retaining only the minimum data necessary for the features you choose, and to anonymising statistical data.

Work records

Projects, sessions, recovery notes and material references are stored on your device. Recording begins when you press Start and follows your capture settings. Original screenshots and end-of-session photos stay on the device. Background records do not store clipboard text.

AI and file tools

AI requests run only when you have configured your own AI service key. Analysis and companion conversations send the necessary OCR text, activity context, messages, project summaries and material information directly to your configured service, currently DeepSeek. When the companion uses file tools, file text, search results and command output may also enter subsequent AI requests to answer your question or carry out an operation you requested.

Screenshot pixels and camera photos are excluded from AI analysis requests. Keys are held in the system credential store. The AI provider processes requests under its own terms.

Accounts and sync

Account services process your email, password hash, device and session information. Sync is off by default. When you enable sync, projects, recovery notes, conversations, preferences and AI keys are end-to-end encrypted on your device; the server stores ciphertext. Original work files, screenshots and photos are excluded from project sync.

The account centre lets you manage devices, consent, exports and account deletion. Deletion has a 14-day grace period. Account and business data are deleted after this period; associated records follow the service cleanup process.

Anonymous statistics and service records

Telemetry is off by default. The statistics endpoint accepts records only with consent, omits account identifiers, filters values resembling email addresses, IP addresses, URLs and file paths, and retains records for 90 days. Information needed for accounts, security audits and email delivery is handled for those purposes separately.

Cloudflare hosts the website, GitHub provides release information, and email services deliver verification and password recovery messages. These providers process the network requests or email data required for those functions.

Browser storage

Public pages store language preferences and the current companion choice; the download page caches public releases. The account centre uses the session cookies and security tokens needed for sign-in.

Retention and control

You manage and delete local projects. Server retention serves accounts, sync, feedback, security and email delivery. Feedback text is end-to-end encrypted. You can withdraw consent, disable sync and request export or deletion through the account centre.
