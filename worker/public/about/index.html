关于 PixoTap

每一次回来，都能接着做。

我们是谁

PixoTap 团队。我们为来回切换课程、项目与创作的人，留住进度、材料和下一步。

陪你理清下一步

选择一个项目，和合拍的伙伴一起整理思路、安排先后，再接着推进。

认真对待你的数据

我们承诺以最少必要的数据处理和留存提供服务，对统计数据进行匿名处理。项目记录保存在设备上；AI 使用你配置的服务，跨设备同步由你开启。

操作与设置见使用说明，数据处理范围见隐私政策。

About PixoTap

A way back to what matters.

Who we are

The PixoTap team. We help people moving between courses, projects and creative work keep their progress, materials and next step together.

Work out what comes next

Choose a project and a companion. Make sense of your thoughts, decide what comes first, and keep moving.

Your data, treated with care

We commit to the minimum data processing and retention needed to provide the service, and anonymise statistical data. Project records stay on your device. AI uses the service you configure; you choose when to enable sync.

See the guide for setup and the Privacy Policy for data handling.
